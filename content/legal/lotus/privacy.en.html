Lotus is a mobile app for keeping track of the series and movies you watch and sharing that with others. It is made by Mercan Software ("we"). This page explains what information we collect, why, and what rights you have over it.

Questions: mercansoftware1@gmail.com

Using the app without an account

You can use Lotus without creating an account. In that case the series, movies and watch history you add are stored only on your phone and are not sent to us.

Information we collect

Account. If you create an account we store your email address. If you sign in with Google, Google gives us your email address, name and profile photo. We never see your password; passwords are kept encrypted by our sign-in service.

Profile. The username, display name, profile photo and bio you choose. If you wish, you can also add your gender, country, year of birth and favourite genres; none of these is required and you can remove them at any time. What is on your profile is visible to other users.

Your watching. The series and movies you track, the episodes you watched and when you marked them, your ratings, notes, lists and the badges you earned. Unless you make your profile private, your watch history and ratings are visible to other users.

What you share. Comments you write, reactions you leave, who you follow and who you block. Comments are public.

Communities. The communities you create or join, your rank in them and the messages you write there. Messages in a community can be seen by its members.

Private messages. Messages you exchange with another user are stored on our server and can be seen only by you and that person. When a report has to be looked into, we may see the content that was reported.

Device. To send you notifications we store your phone's notification identifier (a Firebase Cloud Messaging token) and the app language.

Purchases. If you buy Premium, the payment is handled by Google Play. Your card or payment details never reach us. We store only the purchase token Google provides, the product name and the subscription's expiry date, to verify that the purchase is valid.

What you write to us. Feedback you send from inside the app and reports you file.

Advertising

Users without Premium are shown ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and approximate location (from the IP address) to show and measure ads. This is carried out by Google; details: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before ads are shown. You can turn off ad personalisation or reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Premium members see no ads.

Why we use this information

  • To keep your account and library in sync across your devices.
  • To run the social features: the feed, following, comments, reactions, messages.
  • To send you notifications: new episodes of series you follow, and follows, likes, replies and messages you receive. You can turn notifications off in the app's settings.
  • To verify your Premium membership.
  • To prevent abuse and look into reports.

We do not sell your information.

Services we share information with

We use the following services to run Lotus; each sees only what it needs to do its job:

  • Supabase: accounts and the database. Data is stored on servers in the European Union (Ireland).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivering notifications to your phone.
  • Google Sign-In: if you choose to sign in with your Google account.
  • Google Play Billing: Premium purchases.
  • Google AdMob: advertising.
  • TheTVDB: series and movie information and artwork. Requests are sent to TheTVDB for the titles you search for or open; these requests carry no information about your account.

We may have to share information with authorities when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, watch history, ratings, notes, lists, comments, messages and notification identifiers are deleted for good.

Your rights and deleting your account

  • Access and correction: you can see and change your profile and records inside the app.
  • Privacy: Settings > Account > Private profile hides your library from others.
  • Deletion: Settings > Account > Delete account permanently deletes your account and everything tied to it. If you cannot reach the app, write to mercansoftware1@gmail.com from your account's email address and we will delete it within 30 days. The steps are also on this page: Delete your Lotus account
  • Copy and objection: write to the same address to ask for a copy of your data or to object to how it is used.

If you live in the European Economic Area or in Türkiye, you can exercise your rights under the GDPR and the KVKK through this address.

Children

Lotus is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you notice that a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

The connection between the app and the server is encrypted. The database has rules so that each user can reach only their own private records. No system is completely secure; if you notice a security problem, please write to us.

Changes

If we update this policy we will change the date above. Important changes will also be announced inside the app.
