Lotus is a series and movie tracking app made by Mercan Software. You can delete your account and the data tied to it at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Lotus and go to your profile.
  2. Tap the gear at the top right to open Settings.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm on the warning, then type DELETE to finish.

The account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Write to mercansoftware1@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my Lotus account". We delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your account (email address and sign-in), profile, watch history, ratings, notes, lists, comments, reactions, follows, private messages, community messages and memberships, and your phone's notification identifier are deleted for good. Nothing is kept afterwards.

Purchases are handled by Google Play; the record of a purchase stays in your Google account and is not something we can remove. To delete only part of your data without deleting the account (for example your watch history), write to the same address.
