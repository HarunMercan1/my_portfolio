Pomodoro Elite is a mobile app that helps you focus with the Pomodoro technique and keep track of your study and work time. It is made by Mercan Software ("we"). This page explains what information we collect, why, and what rights you have over it.

Questions: mercansoftware1@gmail.com

Using the app without an account

You can use Pomodoro Elite without creating an account. In that case your settings, themes and focus statistics are stored only on your phone and are not sent to us. They are removed when you uninstall the app.

Information we collect

Account. If you create an account we store your email address. If you sign in with Google, Google gives us your email address, name and a link to your profile photo. We never see your password; passwords are kept encrypted by our sign-in service.

Your focus statistics. If you are signed in, your total number of sessions, total focus time, daily streak and your focus minutes and session counts per day are stored on our server so your progress stays in sync across devices. Statistics you collected as a guest are moved to your account when you sign in.

Purchases. If you buy Premium, the payment is handled by Google Play; your card or payment details never reach us. To verify the purchase and link it to your account, the product you bought, your subscription status and your in-app user ID are kept by RevenueCat.

Technical information. When you download focus music, the files come from our server; as with any internet request, standard connection details such as your IP address are processed.

Notifications. Focus and break notifications are scheduled and shown on your phone; no information is sent to us for this.

Advertising

Users without Premium are shown ads through Google AdMob: banner ads on some screens, full-screen ads at some transitions, and optional rewarded ads for unlocking themes. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and approximate location (from the IP address) to show and measure ads. This is carried out by Google; details: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can turn off ad personalisation or reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Premium members see no ads.

Why we use this information

  • To keep your account and statistics in sync across your devices.
  • To verify your Premium membership and let you restore it on a new device.
  • To support the free version with ads.
  • To answer support requests.

We do not sell your information and we do not send marketing emails.

Services we share information with

We use the following services to run Pomodoro Elite; each sees only what it needs to do its job:

  • Supabase: accounts, storing your statistics, and focus music files.
  • Google Sign-In: if you choose to sign in with your Google account.
  • RevenueCat and Google Play Billing: Premium purchases.
  • Google AdMob: advertising.
  • Google Play: in-app review and update prompts.

We may have to share information with authorities when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. When we delete your account, your email address, sign-in details and all statistics in the cloud are deleted for good. Signing out does not delete your account.

Your rights and deleting your account

  • Access and correction: you can see your statistics in the app; write to us for requests about your account details.
  • Deletion: send an email from your account's email address to mercansoftware1@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Pomodoro Elite account"; we delete the account and everything tied to it within 30 days. The steps are also on this page: Delete your Pomodoro Elite account
  • Copy and objection: write to the same address to ask for a copy of your data or to object to how it is used.

If you live in the European Economic Area or in Türkiye, you can exercise your rights under the GDPR and the KVKK through this address.

Children

Pomodoro Elite is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you notice that a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

The connection between the app and the server is encrypted. The database has rules so that each user can reach only their own records. No system is completely secure; if you notice a security problem, please write to us.

Changes

If we update this policy we will change the date above. Important changes will also be announced inside the app.
