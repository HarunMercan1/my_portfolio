Pomodoro Elite is a focus timer app made by Mercan Software. You can have your account and the data tied to it deleted at any time.

How to delete your account

In the app: sign in to Pomodoro Elite and tap Settings > Delete Account. Type DELETE to confirm. Your account and cloud data are deleted right away and for good. This option is available in version 3.3.5 and later.

By email: if you can't use the app, write to mercansoftware1@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my Pomodoro Elite account". We delete the account within 30 days and let you know when it is done.

What is deleted

Your account (email address, your name and profile photo link if you signed in with Google, and sign-in details) and all your focus statistics in the cloud (total sessions, total time, streak and daily records) are deleted for good.

Data on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app. Purchases are handled by Google Play; the record of a purchase stays in your Google account and is not something we can remove. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription; if you have one, cancel it separately in Google Play. Purchase records that must be kept for legal or fraud-prevention reasons may be kept for the required period. To delete only your statistics without deleting the account, write to the same address.
