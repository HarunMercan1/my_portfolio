Pomodoro Elite is a focus timer app made by Mercan Software. You can have your account and the data tied to it deleted at any time.

How to delete your account

Write to mercansoftware1@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my Pomodoro Elite account". We delete the account within 30 days and let you know when it is done.

What is deleted

Your account (email address, your name and profile photo link if you signed in with Google, and sign-in details) and all your focus statistics in the cloud (total sessions, total time, streak and daily records) are deleted for good. Nothing is kept afterwards.

Data on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app. Purchases are handled by Google Play; the record of a purchase stays in your Google account and is not something we can remove. To delete only your statistics without deleting the account, write to the same address.
