This privacy policy explains how the Wallet Elite mobile application ("App"), developed by Mercan Software, collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data.

1. Data we collect

  • Account information: your email address, your name, your user ID and (if you sign in with Google) a link to your Google profile photo.
  • Profile photo: a photo you may choose from your gallery and upload. Uploading one is optional.
  • Financial records: wallets, balances, income/expense transactions, categories, budgets, recurring transactions and debt records that you enter yourself. The App does not connect to your bank accounts and does not collect card or bank details.
  • Purchase information: if you buy a premium subscription or one-time purchase, the product you bought and your subscription status. Payment is handled by Google Play; we never receive your card details.
  • Device and usage data: advertising ID and other device identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, in-app interactions (e.g. ad views and clicks), diagnostics and crash data. This data is collected by the software of our advertising and purchase service providers.
  • Preferences: language, currency, theme and dark mode preferences.

2. How we use data

  • To create and manage your account, store and sync your records across your devices, and show you statistics and reports.
  • To verify your premium purchases and unlock premium features.
  • To show ads in the free version of the App, measure ad performance and prevent fraud.
  • To detect errors and improve the App.

The financial records you enter in the App are not used for advertising and are not shared with advertising partners. Your data is not sold.

3. Advertising

The free version of the App shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your advertising ID, device information, approximate location and ad interactions to serve, personalise and measure ads. Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent form on first launch; you can change your choice later in the App under Settings → Ad privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Premium users are not shown ads.

4. Data storage and security

Your account information and financial records are stored on the infrastructure of our service provider Supabase. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and protected by access rules so that each user can only access their own data. Your data is kept until your account is deleted.

5. Third-party services

  • Supabase — authentication, database and file storage (privacy policy)
  • Sign in with Google — optional sign-in method; only your name, email address and profile photo are received (privacy policy)
  • Google AdMob — advertising (how Google uses data)
  • RevenueCat — subscription and purchase management (privacy policy)
  • Google Play Billing — payment processing (privacy policy)

6. Account and data deletion

You can delete your account and all of your data at any time:

  • In the App: go to Settings → Delete account. Your account, profile photo and all of your financial records (wallets, transactions, categories, budgets, debts) are deleted immediately and permanently.
  • By email: if you cannot access the App, send an email with the subject "Account deletion request" from your registered email address to mercansoftware1@gmail.com. Your request will be processed within 30 days.

After deletion we do not retain any of your personal data. Records of your purchases on Google Play may continue to be kept by Google under its legal obligations. If you have an active subscription, deleting your account does not cancel it; you need to cancel the subscription in Google Play.

7. Children's privacy

The App is intended for adults; it is not directed at anyone under 18 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always published on this page.
